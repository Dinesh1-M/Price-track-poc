# Current master-spec demo walkthrough

This guide supersedes the earlier Client Partner editing and draft-version instructions. Start with `npm ci`, then `npm start` in price-track-poc; open http://localhost:3000. SQLite is the local default. All identities and integrations are mocks, and financial rules are unapproved.

1. **Demo Client Partner (Excel Sample)**: choose **Salesforce Opportunities** in the top bar. Search/filter the supplied `Salesforce sythetic data.xlsx` workbook, then select an ABC opportunity. Existing cases open rather than duplicating; otherwise the request form is prefilled with mapped opportunity/customer fields. Complete the missing region, delivery model, scope and required dates before creation. The original Opportunity ID is retained and duplicates are checked server-side. This is a local workbook, not a live Salesforce connection. The regular Demo Client Partner is scoped to Northstar Retail, which has no matching rows in this workbook.
2. **Demo Pricing Analyst**: select the new case. In Workflow Tracker advance discovery stages through Proposal in order, recording a reason each time.
3. **Demo BU SPOC**: select the case in Resource Estimates & Pricing Calculator. Add resource with skill/role, grade, location, work type, hours, quantity, periods, utilization, valid dates and hourly cost. Save draft. Optional Autosave resource draft saves revisions without creating versions. Missing skills can be entered manually; there is no HR skill-master screen.
4. If no matching selling rate exists, BU SPOC proposes it in Administration > Local rate administration. Match account/BU/work type/role/grade/location/currency and effective dates. **Demo Finance Reviewer** approves the proposal. The proposer cannot approve their own rate.
5. BU SPOC selects the case, calculates/reviews estimates and clicks **Validate resource estimates**, supplying validation notes. The validation is bound to the current draft revision.
6. Pricing Analyst clicks **Calculate Price**, reviews rate resolution, demo formula basis and KPIs, then **Save Calculation**. Restricted identities do not get cost fields from the backend.
7. Click **Submit working draft**, enter a reason, and submit. This creates formal V1 with immutable snapshot and sequential approval tasks. There is no separate new formal Draft-version creation step. Legacy Draft rows remain historical and are not silently converted.
8. **Demo BU Head** reviews V1 in Approvals and approves. **Demo Finance Reviewer** then approves. Earlier steps cannot be bypassed. Version timeline and comparison shows each step's decision, reviewer, time and comments in the Approval decisions column.
9. To demonstrate parallel editing, edit the working resource draft as BU SPOC while V1 is pending. V1's snapshot and decisions remain unchanged. Submission of a new version is blocked by default while V1 is pending. Administrator may explicitly select demo supersede policy in Administration; this cancels old outstanding tasks and requires fresh approvals. Production supersession policy is pending business approval.
10. For negotiation, Pricing Analyst uses Record discussion with date, requested/offered prices, notes, outcome, next action and discussed version. Save a fresh calculation. BU SPOC validates the new revision, and Pricing Analyst submits a new formal version. Returned/rejected versions are restored to working draft and revised; prior decisions are preserved and are never copied as approvals to the new version.
11. After the current version is fully approved, Pricing Analyst clicks **Mark agreed** and records customer acceptance evidence.
12. Advance to **Contract & Launch** with contract/kickoff evidence, then **Closed Won** with mock Salesforce evidence. The final agreed version must match the current revision. A durable **Pending** Project Track handoff is queued, while case status becomes Closed Won; pending is not accepted.
13. Administrator may dispatch the final Closed Won version through the existing mock handoff dialog and choose success, rejection or timeout. Show the monitor, retry exhaustion exception and successful replay. A duplicate accepted handoff does not create another delivery intake.
14. Administrator can reopen Closed Won with a reason through the API (`POST /api/v1/cases/:id/reopen`). Previous final version/offer/handoff evidence remains. An accepted downstream handoff sets a reconciliation hold; replacement dispatch is blocked. Reconciliation authority and post-Closed-Won amendment authoring are not enabled.

The active case remains selected when switching identities if the new identity can access it; otherwise the workspace moves to the first accessible case. Confirm the case ID before continuing. Changes that increment the draft revision require fresh calculation/resource validation before submission. The validation dialog distinguishes calculation exceptions from pending production Finance approval: demo-mode submissions can proceed through the normal checks while production remains blocked. Refresh after a revision conflict and review the latest values; do not repeatedly overwrite the conflict.

Saved cost changes appear under Negotiations & Pricing Versions > Resource cost change history for cost-visible identities. Historic edits before this feature cannot be reconstructed. Approval evidence appears directly in the matching version timeline row.

The supplied Margin Calculator workbook has been inspected. Its onshore sample multiplies delivery cost by billability, while Price Tracker currently uses scheduled hours for cost; the known sample is therefore not formula parity. Preserve the demo calculation pending Finance decisions on billability, eligible costs, bench loading, vertical GM, IP costs/revenue, rate-card lookups, rounding, discount/COLA and currency. Program/Product production models remain unapproved. In-app notifications are stored locally and are recipient-scoped; no email/Teams delivery or cross-device service exists. See MASTER_SPEC_GAP_ANALYSIS.md and finance/FORMULA_TRACEABILITY.md for evidence and remaining gaps.
